How it works

01

Register

A step-by-step conversation: your basics, appearance, lifestyle, the kind of marriage you are seeking, then the criteria you want in a partner. Your progress is saved, so nothing is lost if you stop and come back.

02

Browse matches

A compatibility score computed from both sides’ preferences, not one side’s. Anyone you have already seen is not shown to you again.

03

Mutual interest

Messaging opens only when each of you has expressed interest in the other. Before that, neither side is contacted.

04

Talk inside the platform

Messages are relayed by the platform itself — in the bot or on the website — so neither of you sees the other’s number or username unless you both decide to share it.

What it offers

🎯

Compatibility scoring

The score comes from mutual preferences — what you want in them and what they want in you — not one generic list shown to everybody.

🌫️

Photo privacy

Your photo is blurred for everyone by default. Revealing it takes an explicit request and your explicit consent, for a duration you choose, revocable at any time.

🛡️

Optional verification

A photo challenge confirms a real person is behind the profile, and the result is visible to the other side. Entirely optional.

💬

In-platform chat

No usernames and no phone numbers change hands. The conversation stays inside the platform — in the bot or on the website — for as long as you want it to.

🔎

Search your way

Quick search, single-criterion search, or a full multi-criteria search when you know exactly what you are looking for.

🌍

Six languages

Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Indonesian and Persian — a complete interface in each, switchable in the bot or on the website.

Privacy is the default, not a setting you hunt for

Your photo is blurred for everyone from the first moment. There is no switch you can forget and be exposed by; the opposite holds — it is revealed only on an explicit request from the other side and explicit consent from you, for a duration you choose (five minutes, a day, or indefinitely), and you can revoke it at any time.

Profiles published to our channel and social pages carry only a first name and a drawn avatar. A real user photo is never published.

You can edit your details or hide your profile entirely at any moment, and every profile carries a report button that reaches a human reviewer.

Frequently asked questions

Is the service free?

Yes. Registration, search, matching and chat after mutual interest are all free. There is an optional premium membership that unlocks extra features, paid with Telegram Stars or cryptocurrency.

Can other people see my photo?

No. Your photo is blurred for everyone by default. It is revealed only if the other side requests it and you explicitly consent, for the duration you choose, and you can revoke that permission at any time.

Does the other person get my phone number or username?

No. Messages are relayed inside the platform itself — in the bot or on the website — so no number and no username is exchanged unless the two of you decide to share it.

How is the compatibility score calculated?

From mutual preferences: each side’s attributes against what the other is looking for — not one side alone. And anyone shown to you once is not shown again.

Is this strictly for marriage?

Yes. The service is for marriage-focused introductions only. Every profile carries a report button that reaches a human reviewer, and anyone who steps outside that is banned.

Which languages are supported?

Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Indonesian and Persian. The interface is complete in all six and can be switched at any time, in the bot or on the website.

How do I start?

Two ways: open the bot in Telegram and press Start — the conversation guides you step by step, and your progress is saved if you stop and come back. Or sign up on the website with a Google, Facebook or Telegram account and complete your profile there.
