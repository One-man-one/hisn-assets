Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This document describes what the Hisn Alzawaj bot collects about you, why, who sees it, how long it stays, and how you delete it. It is written to be read, not signed: no twisted legal phrasing, and no promise that the service itself does not keep.

Who we are and what this covers

Hisn Alzawaj is a service for finding a marriage partner. It runs entirely inside Telegram, through the bot @HisnAlzawaj_bot.

This document covers the bot and this landing page. It does not cover Telegram itself — a separate platform with its own policy, which governs what happens to your account there.

What you give us yourself

Registration asks for the following. You type every answer, and you can change or clear it at any time from "Edit my data":

  • Name, date of birth, gender.
  • Nationality, country, city.
  • Religion and school of thought.
  • Education, occupation, income level.
  • Marital status, children, acceptable marriage types.
  • Physical description, smoking, hijab, languages spoken.
  • A short bio, and the qualities you want in a partner.

Optional fields may be left blank. You appear less often in search results; nothing else is withheld from you.

What Telegram gives us — and what it does not

Telegram passes us three things about your account: your numeric ID, your username if you have one, and your app language.

It does not pass us your phone number, your contacts, or your chat list. Telegram gives bots none of that, so we do not hold it even if we wanted to.

What your use of the service creates

Your last-active time, your likes and matches, reports you send or that reach us about you, and the chat messages exchanged inside the bot — the last of these are deleted automatically after 30 days, detailed below.

Your photo — blurred until you allow it

Your photo is not shown clearly to anyone until you allow that person, request by request. The default everyone sees is a blurred version.

Permission can be temporary, with a viewing window that starts at the first actual opening rather than at the moment you approve — an approval given at 3 a.m. while the requester sleeps must not expire before they see it. Photos shown through a temporary window are deleted from the viewer’s chat once it ends.

You may withdraw permission at any time. Changing your photo revokes every previous permission at once, without your having to ask.

Identity verification — the video is never stored

Verification is entirely optional. You send a short video in which you perform a gesture and show a code issued to you at that moment.

The video is not stored with us in any form: it is downloaded into working memory, sent for automated analysis, then released. Neither the video nor its Telegram identifier is written to our database.

What remains is the decision alone: approved, referred to human review, or rejected — with its reason and its time.

The card published on our pages

To help you reach a partner sooner, we publish an introductory card about you on our channel and official pages. It carries:

  • Your first name only — no family name.
  • The public fields of your profile: age, nationality, marital status and the like.
  • A generic illustrative image based on your gender — never your own photo, not even a blurred copy of it.

No phone number or contact handle of yours is published. Anyone who wants to reach you goes through the bot.

Publishing is on by default once registration is complete, and you can switch it off for yourself at any moment from the "My profile" screen. If you switch it off or delete your account, we take down what was published. Some platforms offer no programmatic deletion, so removal there is manual and may lag slightly.

Chat messages

Messages between two parties pass through the bot and are stored with us for 30 days, then deleted automatically. They are not a permanent archive, and they are not kept beyond that period for any purpose.

There is one reason for this and no other: handling reports. Someone who says they were mistreated cannot be given justice if nothing remains of what was said. Messages are not read except on a report or a suspicion of abuse.

That is why the period is 30 days and no more: someone who was mistreated reports it within days, so keeping messages any longer increases what could be lost in a breach without increasing what gives anyone justice.

Payments

Premium membership is paid with Telegram Stars or with cryptocurrency. Both happen outside our bot, at the provider.

So we never see or store card or wallet details. What stays with us is the transaction identifier, the amount, the provider name and the date — the minimum needed to activate your membership and to request a refund if one is due.

Who sees your data

  • Other users: what appears on your profile and in search results.
  • Service moderators: on a report, a verification review, or a complaint.
  • Telegram: the platform the whole service runs on.
  • Google: automated analysis of the verification video — with no storage on our side, as described above.
  • A machine translation service: translating your bio when someone views it in another language.
  • A cloud hosting provider: running the server and the database.
  • Publishing platforms — Telegram, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, YouTube and TikTok: only the card described above, and only for those who have not switched publishing off.

We do not sell your data, do not hand it to advertisers, and do not build targeted advertising on it. None of that is asked of you in exchange for using the service.

Google is the one operational provider we name here, because the verification video is the most sensitive thing that passes through us — and a promise not to store it is not credible if you do not know who receives it. Anyone who asks for the names of the others may write to us.

How long your data stays

It stays as long as your account does — because the service itself is showing your profile to those who match what you are looking for.

Limited records survive deletion: moderation entries and the payment log, to the extent needed for accounting and to keep a banned account from returning immediately under a new one.

Deleting your account — erasure, not hiding

Use /delete, or the "Delete my account" button on your profile screen. Once you confirm, the following is erased at once:

  • Your entire profile, photo and bio included.
  • Your likes, matches, conversations and their messages.
  • Photo permissions you granted and requested.
  • Your pending notifications and your browsing queue.

At the same time, whatever was published about you on our channel and pages is taken down — deleting the account is a withdrawal of consent, and the card of someone who asked to be erased must not stay on public display.

Your rights, exercised by you

  • Access and correction: "Edit my data" in the menu.
  • Full deletion: the /delete command.
  • Stop publishing on our pages: the "My profile" screen.
  • Stop reminder messages: from the message itself.
  • Withdraw photo permission: the photo privacy screen.
  • A question, complaint, or request the above does not cover: write to us.

None of these requires writing to us or waiting for a reply. They were all made one tap away inside the bot deliberately.

Age

The service is for those aged 18 and over. Any account we find to belong to someone younger is deleted.

Security — and what we do not promise

Connections to our servers are encrypted, and administrative access to data is limited to those who need it for their work.

No system is perfectly secure, so we will not promise what we cannot guarantee. What we do guarantee is twofold: that we do not collect what we do not need, and that the most sensitive thing that ever passes through us — your verification video — is never stored at all.

Changes to this document

If what we do changes, we change this document and update the date at its top. A material change is announced on our channel, not slipped in here quietly.

Contact

Write to us inside the bot @HisnAlzawaj_bot — the fastest route, since your account is already known there and you need to prove nothing.

You will also find us on our channel @Hisn_Alzawaj.
